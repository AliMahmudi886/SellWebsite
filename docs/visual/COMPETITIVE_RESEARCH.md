# Competitive research

- [Finnish Design Shop](https://www.finnishdesignshop.com/en-us/) pairs product discovery with design stories and live search results.
- [IKEA](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/) makes browsing by household use and jumping past long listings easy.
- [Basalam](https://basalam.com/) prioritizes search and cart access in Persian and puts buyer information close to shopping.
- [Awwwards commerce index](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/e-commerce/) was reviewed as a discovery reference for editorial storefronts.

The store applies category-led discovery, a brand story between commerce sections, and direct links to delivery and return policies. No third-party designs or assets were imported.
