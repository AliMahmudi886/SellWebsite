# Brand direction

We considered a neutral gallery, a saturated boutique and a warm editorial shop. The warm editorial direction gives the linen, ceramics and baskets a shared visual context while preserving useful contrast for commerce controls.

Use dark espresso for the framing surfaces, cream for browsing, and fired clay for primary actions. Keep Vazirmatn and use the existing self-hosted lifestyle photography. Let the product categories and delivery information carry the navigation.
